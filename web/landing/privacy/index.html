Privacy information

The operator must publish an approved privacy notice before accepting public orders.

Pilot data handling

The application uses saved delivery addresses, order records and account details to operate deliveries. Rider tracking is permitted only for active assigned deliveries; completed deliveries lose access to customer address details.

Account deletion is available in the customer app. Financial records may require retention determined by the operator and legal advice.

This development notice is not a reviewed legal policy. The operator must specify its legal identity, contact, processors, retention periods and rights process before launch.
