Cancellations and refund requests

Order cancellation eligibility depends on its current server state.

Request a review

Open the order in the customer app and choose Help with this order, then Refund request. An operator reviews the request against the applicable policy and records the outcome.

Eligible paid order cancellations use the configured payment gateway refund flow. The order screen shows the recorded refund state. Automated vendor or rider disbursements are not enabled.

Before launch, the operator must approve and publish timing, eligibility, exceptions and statutory rights.
